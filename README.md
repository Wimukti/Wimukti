### Building: [ReviewStage](https://github.com/Wimukti/reviewstage)

**AI wrote the PR. You still have to review it.** ReviewStage is an open-source pull-request
review assistant on Claude Code. It drafts the review from the real diff on your own Claude
plan, stages every finding privately, and posts only what you pick — as you, never as a bot.
Nothing posts until you click.

<a href="https://github.com/Wimukti/reviewstage"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Wimukti/reviewstage/main/docs/demos/review.gif" alt="ReviewStage: paste a PR, run the review, keep two findings, post them as yourself" width="720"></a>

```bash
npx reviewstage
```

Desktop app for Mac and Linux · your phone pairs by QR code · push notifications · swipe to keep
a finding · learns what your team drops · MIT ·
[70-second demo](https://youtu.be/yYMtQK-sVyg)
